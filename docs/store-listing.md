## Full description [4000]

Your workouts are a dungeon crawl.

Every training day you show up, your hero fights through a room of a 3D
dungeon. Every personal record breaks a gate seal and opens the stairs down.
Every fifth floor a miniboss blocks the way with a challenge built from your
own numbers, one attempt a week. And at the bottom waits a dragon whose ward
only breaks when you reach your real goal: the body weight, the lift or the
consistency you set out to hit.

TRAIN WITH A REAL PLAN
• A program built for your goal (build muscle, get fitter, lose fat), your
  experience and your training days
• Full exercise sets you can customise, or follow as recommended
• "I don't have this equipment" swaps that keep the same movement, so push,
  pull and every muscle stays covered
• Home and body weight workouts so there is never an excuse
• Start and end every set to time it; faster sets earn rewards too

PLAY A REAL GAME
• Live 3D battles: every set is a strike, records land as critical hits
• Loot chests with rarity-coloured beams: common to legendary gear that
  changes how your hero looks
• Level up, raise Strength, Endurance, Agility and Vitality
• Streaks, potions, titles and a merchant
• A victory sequence after every workout that counts up everything you earned

TRAIN SMART, RECOVER BETTER
• Warnings before weekly volume outruns your recovery, based on published
  training research
• A 30 second check-in on sleep, energy, stress and soreness that trims the
  plan on rough days instead of skipping them
• Load-spike and recovery-window warnings, safe body weight pace guidance and
  protein, water and sleep targets for your body
• Rest timers with alerts

NEVER MISS A SESSION
• Reminders at your gym time on training days
• Move today's session earlier or later, or swap it with a rest day

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Training guidance is general fitness information, not medical advice.
