# Ironwyrm privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Ironwyrm is a workout tracker that turns your training into a dungeon crawler.
This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short
version: everything stays on your phone.

## What the app stores

To plan workouts and award progress, Ironwyrm keeps the following on your
device:

- Your hero name, chosen class and game progress (level, gear, dungeon floor).
- Training details you enter: body weight, experience level, goal, training
  days, gym time and available equipment.
- Workout history: exercises, sets, reps, weights and set times.
- Daily check-in answers about sleep, energy, stress, soreness and joint pain.
- Body weight entries.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. Ironwyrm has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no
advertising and no third-party tracking. The app does not collect, sell or
share personal or health data.

Gym reminders are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. They are
not sent through any server.

## Backups and deletion

- **Export:** Settings, then "Export a backup", creates a text copy of your
  data that you choose where to send or save. Ironwyrm never sends it anywhere
  on its own.
- **Delete:** Settings, then "Start over", erases everything from the device.
  Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** only to remind you at your gym time and when rest
  between sets ends. You can turn them off at any time.
- **Vibration:** for haptic feedback during workouts.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

## Health information

Training guidance in the app is general fitness information, not medical
advice. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new program if you
have a health condition, and stop if something hurts beyond normal training
soreness.

## Children

Ironwyrm is not directed at children under 13.
